EmoDict v0.3.1 — Complete Architecture

Local-first dictation with emotion-aware tone tags · every module, every handoff, every decision · Windows · 100% on-device

Module Reference — every file, what it does, what it hands over

Hook & Orchestration src/main.py

  • Role: the conductor. Binds the hotkey, spawns the worker per take, owns shutdown safety.
  • Threading: hook thread → PortAudio thread → 1 worker per take → tkinter main thread. Dictation never blocks the UI.
  • Shutdown guard: no recording starts and no paste happens after quit begins — a finishing worker re-checks the flag right before injecting.
  • Parallel fork: starts emotion scoring the same instant Whisper starts; joins both before assembly.

Capture src/recorder.py

  • Engine: PortAudio via sounddevice. One persistent stream, 16kHz mono float32.
  • Pre-roll ring (0.3s): always recording into a ring; the take starts with audio from just before your keypress.
  • Tail drain (0.2s): on release, waits for buffered audio so your last word lands.
  • Output: one NumPy float32 array in RAM. Nothing is written to disk (unless EMO_KEEP_AUDIO=1 debug mode).

Speech Engine src/transcriber.py

  • Engine: faster-whisper (CTranslate2 reimplementation of OpenAI Whisper).
  • Models: tiny/base/small/medium/large-v3-turbo; auto-picked by hardware probe. base = 10x realtime on a normal CPU; turbo needs GPU (8x faster there).
  • Inside: Silero VAD (onnx) strips silence → Mel spectrogram → transformer → beam=1 decode.
  • Output: sentences with timestamps. Never sees prosody — it normalizes away how you spoke.

Emotion Engine src/emotion.py

  • Engine: emotion2vec_plus_base (funasr + PyTorch, CPU), trained self-supervised on 262h of emotional speech; 77.6% on IEMOCAP, cross-language.
  • Input: the raw audio array — the whole take, never the text, never per sentence (measured: signal collapses when chopped).
  • Output: 9 probabilities → one decision via calibration + memory.
  • Blind spot by design: doesn't know the words. Sarcasm needs both engines (future LLM merge).

Calibration src/calibration.py

  • Role: your personal thresholds, measured from your labeled clips.
  • Why: stock threshold 0.6 is calibrated on acted shouting; real desk-voice anger scores 0.1–0.2 for most people while their calm baseline is ~0.005.
  • Evidence: founder calm ≤0.008 vs frustrated 0.123 → threshold 0.04. First live hit: [tone: angry, 0.88].
  • File: %APPDATA%\EmoDict\calibration.json.

Voice Memory src/voice_memory.py

  • Role: the learning layer. Every take updates rolling mean ± std (Welford) per emotion class — a fingerprint of YOUR voice.
  • Rule: after 8 takes per class, a score z>4 above your personal baseline = emotion, regardless of absolute values.
  • Effect: self-calibrating with use; no labeling sessions needed after cold start.
  • File: %APPDATA%\EmoDict\voice_memory.json.

Assembly src/cleanup.py

  • Steps: strip fillers (um/uh) → dictionary corrections with word boundaries ("flyt base"→"Flytbase") → sentence case → attach the tag once at the front.
  • Always-tag (v0.3.1): every take carries a tone tag, including [tone: neutral] — the receiving AI never wonders whether tone was measured.
  • Output: the final string: [tone: angry, 0.88] your words…

Delivery src/injector.py

  • Mechanism: save your clipboard → put text on it → send Ctrl+V → restore your clipboard (<1s exposure, disclosed).
  • Raw Win32 via ctypes with explicit 64-bit pointer prototypes.
  • Works in any app — the tag arrives wherever your cursor is, including AI chats, which is the point.

Experience Layer overlay.py · mainwindow.py · tray.py · wizard_gui.py · welcome.py

  • Overlay: red ● recording / amber … transcribing chip; auto-hides after 15s no matter what (failsafe).
  • Main window: visible face on every launch — hold-key instruction, engine status, searchable ledger of every take.
  • Tray: Settings / Pause / Dashboard / Updates / Quit.
  • GUI onboarding: hardware probe, model pick, live mic level bar, one-click install. The console wizard once crashed every packaged first-run.

Analytics stats.py · dashboard.py

  • Dashboard: local-only web page (127.0.0.1:8417, stdlib server, auto-refresh).
  • Metrics: takes, words, avg/p95/max latency, real-time factor, failed takes, tone-tag rate, takes/day chart, recent-takes table.
  • Source: history.log v2 — every take: timestamp | latency | audio-length | tone | text.

Safety & Ops crash.py · update_check.py · selftest.py · settings_store.py · hardware.py

  • Crash capture: every unhandled exception on any thread → crash.log; failed takes are logged and skipped, never kill the app.
  • Selftest: EmoDict.exe --selftest runs the full pipeline on a bundled wav and proves BOTH branches (words + tone) — used to gate every release.
  • Update check: prompt-only, compares VERSION.txt on GitHub.
  • Settings: %APPDATA%\EmoDict\settings.json; survives updates; 0.3.0 migration auto-enables emotion for upgraders.
